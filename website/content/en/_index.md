## What this site covers

- **Servers**: server-class systems, boards, workstations, cloud instances and HPC clusters, one entry per model
- **Chips**: server-class SoCs and CPU IP, with cores, ISA and intended use spelled out
- **Solutions**: distributions, virtualisation, containers, HPC, databases and inference services, separating what already runs from what is missing
- **News**: a summary and a link to the original, every item checked to open

## What gets in

Every entry has to point back to a primary source: a vendor product page, datasheet, release note or official repository. Second-hand reporting is labelled as such; unknown specifications stay blank instead of being guessed.
